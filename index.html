NielloPlayer Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 3, 2026

NielloPlayer is a local music player. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data — music, lyrics, playlists, settings, playback history, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers — with a developer or third-party server.

The app accesses local audio after your permission, to scan and play it. Its index, queue, and settings remain on the device. Folders you select are accessed only through Android's system file picker. Wi-Fi, web, and hotspot transfer run only when you start them and send files directly over the local network; files never pass through NielloPlayer servers. Bluetooth is used only for headphone playback and sharing you initiate, not for device scanning.

Uninstalling the app deletes its index, queue, and settings. Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer email listed under Developer contact on the Google Play listing.
